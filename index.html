Our Story

Design With Purpose

KNR Interiors was born from a simple belief: every home deserves to be beautiful, and every family deserves a space that truly feels like their own. Founded by Jagadeeswar Reddy Karna, our studio has transformed hundreds of homes across Telangana,Andhra Pradesh and Banglore.

We blend timeless aesthetics with Vastu principles to create spaces that are not just visually stunning, but also filled with positive energy, peace, and harmony. Our approach is deeply personalized — we listen, we plan, and we deliver beyond expectations.

From a 2BHK apartment to a luxury villa, every project receives the same level of dedication, craftsmanship, and care. Budget-friendly doesn't mean compromising on quality — it means smarter design.
